## Project context
DripUp is an AI commerce coworker for Shopify fashion sellers. This repo is a single-scroll Next.js 15 landing page (Pages Router, React 19, Tailwind CSS v4, GSAP + ScrollTrigger, Three.js WebGL dissolve). The page tells the product story through a hero section with scroll-driven WebGL dissolve, animated chat panel, and word swap heading — followed by 5 stacked feature cards that animate on scroll and expand to a full-screen CTA. The active branch is `main`. Full design rules are in `DESIGN.md`. Full change history is in `PROJECT_LOG.md`.
